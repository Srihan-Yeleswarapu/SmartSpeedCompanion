# Ad-Gated Feature Ideas — SmartSpeedCompanion

> *Five hand-picked features to unlock by watching a single rewarded ad.*

---

## 3. Offline Map Region Download (24h)

**Watch an ad to download a 50 km² map region for offline navigation for 24 hours.**

- **Why it works:** Rural areas, tunnels, and spotty cell coverage are universal pain points for navigation apps. A time-limited offline unlock respects server storage costs while delivering real, tangible utility during the drive.
- **Unlock duration:** 24 hours from download.
- **User flow:**
  1. User taps "Download Offline Region" on a map area.
  2. Ad plays (rewarded video).
  3. Region downloads and is usable offline for 24 hours.
  4. After expiry, user can watch another ad to refresh or extend.

## 9. Trip Fuel Cost Estimator

**Watch an ad to get a fuel cost estimate for your trip based on distance, average speed, and your vehicle's MPG (user-set in a profile).**

- **Why it works:** Practical, budget-friendly value. Commuters and cost-conscious drivers will use this repeatedly — creating a steady stream of repeat ad views.
- **User flow:**
  1. User sets their vehicle's fuel efficiency (MPG/L/100km) in profile settings (free, one-time).
  2. Before or after a trip, user taps "Estimate Fuel Cost".
  3. Ad plays.
  4. App shows: estimated fuel used (gallons/liters), estimated cost (at local average fuel price), and a comparison vs. alternative routes.
- **Optional enhancement:** Show CO₂ emissions alongside cost for eco-conscious drivers.

## 10. Extended Trip History Access

**Watch an ad to unlock and browse trips older than 7 days (otherwise auto-archived/summarized).**

- **Why it works:** Creates natural scarcity — trips are valuable personal data. Users who want to revisit a specific road trip from three weeks ago will happily watch an ad for that nostalgic deep-dive.
- **Unlock duration:** 24-hour exploratory access to the full archive.
- **User flow:**
  1. User opens Trip History and taps "View Older Trips".
  2. Ad plays.
  3. Full archive (older than 7 days) becomes browsable for 24 hours.
  4. Trips older than 30 days can also be searched/exported during this window.

## 13. Custom Vehicle Icon

**Watch an ad to swap your blue navigation dot/arrow for a high-detail car model or fun icon (sports car, truck, scooter, etc.).**

- **Why it works:** Pure delight and personalization. Users love expressing identity through their navigation icon. Fun, low-stakes, visually obvious — and makes the app feel premium.
- **Unlock duration:** Permanent (one-and-done per icon), or 7-day rental for the premium animated models.
- **User flow:**
  1. User opens Vehicle Icon Shop.
  2. Taps desired icon (e.g., "Red Sportscar" or "Classic Pickup").
  3. Ad plays.
  4. Icon is unlocked and immediately applied to the map.
- **Ideas for icon catalog:**
  - Sports car (red/blue/yellow variants)
  - Classic pickup truck
  - Retro convertible
  - Electric SUV
  - Motorcycle / scooter
  - Monster truck (fun option)
  - Hot air balloon (whimsical)
  - Your current car model (premium, requires vehicle selection)

## 20. Personalized Speed Alert Profiles

**Watch an ad to enable per-road speed alert customizations — e.g., "Alert me at 5 over on highways but at exactly the limit in school zones."**

- **Why it works:** Power-user feature that doesn't gate safety (the default alerts still work). Drivers who want fine-grained control will watch ads to build custom profiles for different driving contexts.
- **Unlock duration:** Permanent per profile (user can create multiple profiles via additional ad views).
- **User flow:**
  1. User navigates to Speed Alerts → Custom Profiles.
  2. Taps "Create New Profile".
  3. Ad plays.
  4. User configures threshold over/under per road type:
     - Highways: +5 mph over limit
     - Residential: +3 mph over limit
     - School zones: exactly at limit
     - Work zones: exactly at limit
     - Custom roads (user-defined): user's choice
  5. Profile is saved and can be activated from the main drive screen.
- **Bonus:** Allow naming the profile ("Weekend Cruise", "Daily Commute", "Road Trip") for easy switching.

## O-modified. Custom Named Locations

**Watch an ad to create a name for an address — "Mom's House," "My Favorite Trailhead," "That One Diner With the Good Pie."**

- **Why it works:** Tiny delight with outsized emotional value. Users remember places by names, not coordinates. Every named location makes the app feel more like *their* map and less like a generic GPS.
- **Unlock duration:** Permanent per saved location name. Additional ad views unlock more naming slots.
- **User flow:**
  1. User taps any location on the map or in their recent history.
  2. Taps "Name This Place."
  3. Ad plays.
  4. User types a custom name ("Mom's House").
  5. The name appears on the map, in search, and in trip history going forward.

## 15. Alert Snooze / "I Know" Button

**Watch an ad to unlock a 15-second snooze for the speeding alert when you tap the "I Know" button. Free version has no snooze at all. You get one snooze per ad watched.**

- **Why it works:** Sometimes you're deliberately going slightly faster to merge, pass, or navigate a tricky situation. The snooze acknowledges the user's judgment without abandoning the safety mission entirely — you get 15 seconds, then the beep comes back if you're still over. This is a classic "respect the user's context" feature that creates goodwill and repeat ad views.
- **Unlock model:** One 15-second snooze per ad watched. You cannot stack or chain snoozes.
- **User flow:**
  1. Speed alert fires (beep + visual flash).
  2. User sees a new "I Know" button on the alert overlay.
  3. Tapping "I Know" triggers a rewarded ad.
  4. After the ad, the alert is silenced for exactly 15 seconds.
  5. If user is *still* speeding after 15 seconds, the beep resumes as normal.
  6. User must watch another ad to get a fresh snooze.
- **Key constraint:** Snooze only works while actively driving. It expires if the car stops.

## 18. Ultra-Minimal "Drive Focus" Mode

**Watch an ad to unlock a dashboard that shows ONLY your speed and the speed limit. No map, no buttons, no navigation prompts, no distractions. When you want to drive, not interact.**

- **Why it works:** The app's core mission is reducing speeding — and nothing serves that mission like stripping away every possible distraction. This mode turns the phone into a pure heads-up display. It's the antithesis of feature creep, and power users who want to focus on the road will gladly watch an ad for it.
- **Unlock duration:** 24 hours per ad.
- **User flow:**
  1. From the main drive screen, user taps "Drive Focus" button.
  2. Ad plays.
  3. Screen instantly transforms: full-screen speedometer with just two numbers — current speed (large) and speed limit (smaller, below).
  4. All other UI is hidden or dimmed: no map, no music controls, no navigation bar, no toolbar buttons.
  5. Alerts still work (they're safety-critical) — but they appear as simple unobtrusive pulses, not full-screen takeovers.
  6. User can exit Focus Mode with a long-press or deliberate gesture.
  7. Focus Mode persists in CarPlay too.

## 19. Multi-Car Profiles

**Watch an ad to add a second vehicle profile with separate buffer settings and stats. For households with two cars, or a work car vs personal car.**

- **Why it works:** Many users drive more than one vehicle — a commuter car and a weekend car, or a truck for work and an SUV for family trips. Each vehicle has different speed characteristics, different alert tolerances, and the user wants separate stats. This is a practical, power-user feature that feels premium without being niche.
- **Unlock model:** One ad unlocks the second vehicle profile permanently. Additional ad views unlock more slots (up to a reasonable max like 4–5).
- **User flow:**
  1. User goes to Settings → Vehicle Profiles.
  2. Taps "Add Vehicle".
  3. Ad plays.
  4. User names the vehicle ("Work Truck", "Family SUV") and configures:
     - Speed buffer: separate thresholds per road type
     - Alert sound preference
     - Vehicle icon on map
     - Whether alerts are enabled at all
  5. Stats are tracked separately per vehicle: trip history, score, streak, etc.
  6. User selects which vehicle they're driving from a quick-access dropdown on the drive screen.
  7. Switching vehicles mid-drive is gated (for safety) — must select before starting the car.
