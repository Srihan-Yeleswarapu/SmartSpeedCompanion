What we can help with

  • Bug reports — describe what happened, where, and which iPhone / iOS version you were on. Screenshots are very helpful.
  • Account questions — signing in, Sign in with Apple, password resets, deleting your account and cloud data.
  • Drive sessions — recording problems, missing trips, errors importing or exporting.
  • Speed-limit accuracy in Arizona — tell us the road, the speed you saw, and what the sign actually says. We refresh the underlying dataset periodically.
  • CarPlay — pairing issues, what shows up on the dash, audio alerts.
  • Feedback & feature suggestions.

Privacy inquiries

Privacy questions, account-deletion requests, and California (CCPA / CPRA) rights requests are answered through the same channels above — please use the subject line Speedio Privacy so they route quickly. The full policy is in our Privacy Policy.

Tip

Please include your iPhone model and iOS version in your first message — this lets us reproduce most issues on the same setup and respond faster.
