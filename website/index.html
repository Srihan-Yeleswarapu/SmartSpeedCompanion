Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 5, 2026

Speedio ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how it is used.

Data We Collect

Location Data

Speedio uses GPS location to display your current speed, show speed limits, and enable CarPlay navigation. If you sign in and record a drive session, GPS coordinates are saved locally on your device. If you choose to sign in with an account, location data may also be synced to our secure cloud database to enable cross-device access and future features. You can stop recording at any time.

Account Information

If you create an account, we collect your email address and a username of your choice. This information is used only for authentication purposes.

Drive Session Data

When you record a drive session, we capture speed readings including GPS coordinates, speed, and speed limit at one-second intervals. This data is stored on your device and, if you are signed in, may be synced to our cloud database. You can delete individual sessions or all data at any time within the app.

Settings Preferences

App preferences (e.g., speed buffer, audio alerts, units) are stored locally and may be synced to your account if you are signed in, so your settings persist across devices.

Crash Detection

Crash detection uses the device's motion coprocessor entirely on-device. No motion or accelerometer data is transmitted anywhere.

How We Use Your Data

  • Display your current speed and speed limits on screen and in CarPlay
  • Provide turn-by-turn navigation and audio alerts
  • Record and display your drive session history (optionally backed up to your account)
  • Sync preferences across devices if you are signed in
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs

Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with any third parties. Your location data, drive sessions, and account information are never sold, rented, or shared with advertisers, analytics companies, or any other external party. Data synced to the cloud is stored securely and used solely to provide you with account-based features.

Third-Party Services

Speedio uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google) — Used for authentication and cloud data storage (Firestore). Firebase Analytics is disabled. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Apple CarPlay — Provides in-car display of app content. Data handling is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

The speed limit database is bundled with the app and does not require network access. No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs are used.

Data Retention & Deletion

Drive sessions remain on your device until you delete them. Account-related cloud data can be deleted by contacting us. You can delete all recorded sessions at any time within the app settings.

Your Choices

You can control location access through your device Settings at any time. You can also sign out of your account to stop cloud syncing. Disabling location will limit core speed display and navigation features.

Children's Privacy

Speedio is rated 4+ on the App Store and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date.
