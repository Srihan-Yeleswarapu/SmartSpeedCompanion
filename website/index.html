Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 5, 2026

Speedio (the "App") is developed by SpeedSense ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information when you use our App.

Information We Collect

Location Data

The App collects precise GPS location data to display your current speed, provide speed limit information, and enable navigation features. Location data is processed on-device and may be used to record drive sessions if you choose to start a session.

Crash Detection Data

The App uses the device motion coprocessor to detect potential crash events (sudden deceleration). This data is processed locally on your device and is not shared unless you explicitly report an incident.

Usage Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the App. This includes app opens, feature usage, and crash reports. This data does not identify you personally.

How We Use Your Information

  • Display your current speed and speed limit information
  • Provide CarPlay navigation and driving alerts
  • Record and display drive session history (when enabled)
  • Detect and alert you to speed cameras and potential crashes
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs

Data Storage & Sharing

Drive session data is stored locally on your device using SwiftData. We do not sell your personal data. Anonymous analytics may be shared with Firebase (Google) for analytics purposes. We do not share your location data with third parties.

Third-Party Services

The App integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google) – Analytics, Authentication, and Crashlytics. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Apple CarPlay – In-car display of app content. Data handling is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Data Retention

Drive sessions are stored on your device until you delete them. You can delete individual sessions or clear all data through the App settings. Analytics data is retained by Firebase in accordance with their data retention policy.

Your Choices

You can control location access and motion tracking through your device Settings. You may also delete drive session data at any time within the App. Disabling location access will limit core functionality.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 4. We do not collect data from anyone regardless of age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
